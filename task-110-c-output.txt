Temporal allocation model:
RentalAllocation is the canonical tenant-scoped temporal projection of a Lease, Booking or OperationalBlock. ACTIVE claims block time; RELEASED claims remain historical and stop blocking.

Operational block model:
OperationalBlock is first-class persisted data with CLEANING, MAINTENANCE, OWNER_USE, ADMINISTRATIVE_BLOCK, PREPARATION, INSPECTION, RENOVATION and OTHER reasons, optional provenance/note, lifecycle status and timestamps. Inspections block time only through an explicit OperationalBlock.

Interval convention:
UTC half-open intervals [startsAt, endsAt). Boundary-touching intervals do not overlap. Lease allocations may be open-ended; bookings and blocks are bounded.

Short-term availability:
Confirmed short-term allocations block only overlapping requested intervals. Free time before, between and after bookings remains allocatable.

Long-term availability:
Future long-term allocations block overlaps. Effective ACTIVE long-term occupancy blocks incompatible allocation, supports an open end and returns REMOVE_FROM_RENTAL_CATALOG until ended or released.

Catalog semantics:
Property Management owns publication. Rental Operations returns AVAILABLE_FOR_INTERVAL, UNAVAILABLE_FOR_INTERVAL or REMOVE_FROM_RENTAL_CATALOG without duplicating or mutating publication state.

Next availability:
The duration-aware engine orders active conflicts and returns the first sufficient gap, rather than MAX(endAt).

Whole-building/unit protection:
A Property Management resolver supplies tenant-validated eligibility and conflict scopes. Whole-building allocation claims the building and its units; unit allocation claims only that unit, so sibling units remain independent.

Concurrency protection:
One rental_allocation_claim row is written per conflict scope in the allocation transaction. A PostgreSQL GiST exclusion constraint over tenant, scope and tstzrange(..., '[)') guarantees that exactly one incompatible concurrent allocation succeeds.

RLS:
Allocations, operational blocks and claims are tenant-scoped with forced RLS. Repositories use trusted tenant-scoped PostgreSQL transactions.

Migration:
0001_unified_temporal_availability.sql adds lifecycle/provenance fields, safe backfills, composite source FKs, claims, indexes, forced RLS/grants, btree_gist and the active-overlap exclusion constraint.
